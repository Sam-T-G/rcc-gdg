Some of you haven’t been to a DevFest, so here’s what it is.

What DevFest is

DevFest is a one-day tech conference run by GDG chapters.

Talks from Googlers, hands-on coding workshops, and people in tech to meet.

Google’s DevFest page

From Google’s DevFest page and FAQ (developers.google.com/community/devfest, checked 2026-11-12): DevFest is “a global, community-led tech conference series hosted by Google Developer Groups.” Google calls it the world’s largest community-driven tech conference. Each DevFest is organized by its local GDG, so every city’s agenda is different. This year’s season runs October 1 to December 31, 2026, and the events are open to everyone.

GDG means Google Developer Group: local clubs for people who build with Google’s tools. We’re a GDG on Campus, the college version. A Google Developer Expert (GDE) is someone outside Google that Google recognizes as an expert in one area, like AI or Cloud.

The banner is DevFest LA’s own, cropped to leave out the photo of a person. QR goes to Google’s page with the map of every DevFest.

Get into GEAR · Google Cloud Career Launchpad

11hours

of hands-on Google Cloud and Gemini lessons on Google Skills, Google’s course site. Finish 90% by Nov 20 and you get invited to a career panel with Googlers.

From Google’s emails to GDG on Campus organizers (October newsletter and the 2026-11-10 reminder): Get into GEAR is this fall’s main campaign for GDG on Campus in North America, September 1 to November 20. Chapters run hands-on workshops, and members work through 4 learning paths on Google Skills, about 11 hours total. It covers how companies build with Google Cloud and the new Gemini features.

Anyone who finishes at least 90% by November 20 gets invited to a virtual career panel and Q&A with Googlers in December.

How we’d join: the chapter posts a Get into GEAR event on Bevy, members register there, and Google sends us a free-access link to share. Google asks for that request 5 to 6 weeks before the event.
