# Code of Conduct

GDG on Campus @ RCC adopts Google's event policy by reference and adds RCC-specific reporting routes. This applies to every chapter meeting, workshop, trip, online space (Discord, Bevy, Instagram comments), and anything done in the club's name.

## The policy we follow

| Document | URL | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| Google Event Community Guidelines and Anti-Harassment Policy | <https://www.google.com/events/policy/anti-harassmentpolicy.html> | Expected behavior, definition of harassment, consequences |
| Same policy, the alias Bevy links as "Community Guidelines" | <https://developers.google.com/community-guidelines> | Redirects to the page above |
| GDG Event Participation Terms | <https://gdg.community.dev/participation-terms/> | Binds anyone on gdg.community.dev or at a GDG event, organizers included; the clause that lets a chapter remove someone from events and the platform |

Google's policy applies to all participants (attendees, partners, sponsors, volunteers, event staff) across talks, workshops, codelabs, social media, parties, and hallway conversations. Its three rules, in Google's words:

1. Be excellent to each other.
2. Speak up if you see or hear something.
3. Practice saying "Yes and" to each other.

Harassment under that policy includes intimidation, stalking, harassing photography, disruption of events, unwelcome sexual attention, and sexualized imagery. The Participation Terms require everyone to "maintain a friendly, safe, supportive and harassment-free environment," and say violators "may be exited from the GDG(s), Site(s) or resources, including future event participation."

Read the full policy. This page summarizes it and does not replace it.

## Recording notice

By RSVPing to a Bevy event, participants agree to the Participation Terms, which authorize recording and let Google use recorded content. Organizers say this out loud at the start of any recorded session and give people a chance to sit out of frame.

## How to report at RCC

Google's policy says to contact event staff. At RCC, event staff means the people below. Names are on file with Student Activities, and organizers are listed by name on the Bevy chapter page; they are not repeated here because this repo is public and rosters in it hold counts only.

| Route | Who | When to use it |
|---|---|---|
| On-site organizer | Any chapter organizer running the event (President, Vice President, Secretary, or the listed Bevy organizer) | First stop at any meeting or event. They can pause the event, separate people, and escalate. |
| President | [TBD: name on consent] | If the on-site organizer is the problem, or after the event |
| Faculty Advisor | [TBD: name] | If any officer is involved, or if you want an adult who is not a student |
| Student Activities Office | <studentactivities@rcc.edu> | Formal complaints, anything involving college policy, or if you do not trust the club to handle it |
| RCC College Safety and Police | [TBD: phone number] | Safety threats, theft, or anything physical |
| Emergency | 911 | Immediate danger |

You can report by any of these routes; you do not have to start with the club. You can bring a friend. You can ask that your name stay out of it, and organizers will honor that as far as the college's own rules allow.

## What organizers do with a report

1. Make sure everyone is safe right now. Separate people if needed. Call College Safety or 911 if there is a threat.
2. Listen, write down what was reported (date, event, what happened, who was told), and keep that note off this repo and off Discord.
3. Tell the President and the Faculty Advisor the same day, unless one of them is the subject of the report. Then tell the other, and Student Activities.
4. Decide on a response. Options under Google's policy range from a warning to removal from the event with no refund and exclusion from future events. Under the Participation Terms the chapter can remove someone from Bevy events and resources.
5. Follow up with the reporter within one week on what was done.
6. Log that a report was received and resolved, without names or details, in the semester retrospective so the next team knows the process was used.

## Applying to online spaces

Discord and Bevy comments follow the same policy. Organizers with moderation rights can delete content, mute, or remove accounts. A removal from Discord for conduct reasons follows the same steps above.
