# Calendar - Fall 2026

Every dated thing this semester, in one table. Deadlines that repeat every year are pre-seeded with `[TBD]` dates. Replace the date when you know it, mark the status `n/a` if the row does not apply this term, and add rows for anything else with a date.

`scripts/new-event.sh` appends a row here for each new event folder. Keep the comment line under the table.

Types: `deadline`, `meeting`, `event`, `training`, `milestone`, `break`. Status: `planned`, `submitted`, `done`, `n/a`.

Academic dates below are from the RCCD 2026-2027 academic calendar (Fall 2026: classes begin August 24; holidays September 7 and November 11; no classes November 23 to 29; finals December 12 to 18; full-term classes end December 18). Google program windows are from the program pages as of August 2026. Club dates are `[TBD]` until the team sets them.

**The weekly slot is Thursdays, 2:30 to 3:30 PM, BLCIS A-210 Simulation Lab.** Both campus holidays fall on a Monday and a Wednesday, so neither costs a meeting. Only November 26 (no-class week) and December 17 (finals) are dark. Every meeting below has a facilitator script in [sessions/](sessions/README.md).
