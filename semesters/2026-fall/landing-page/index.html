Voice

Find the sentence that gets you in the room.

Someone who can help you asks what you do. Here is one answer, three times.

Pick a major

Nursing

  1. 20 secondsUm, so I’m a nursing major, I’m taking anatomy and pharmacology right now, and I kind of work part time at the campus clinic, and I’ve been helping them fix their paperwork, which has honestly been pretty interesting.All of that is there for you. None of it is for them.
  2. 12 secondsI’m a nursing major, and I work part time at the campus clinic, and I’ve been helping them fix their paperwork, which has been pretty interesting.Same answer with the hedging and the course list gone.
  3. 8 secondsI work at the campus clinic, where check-in took eleven minutes. I rewrote the intake form, and now it’s four.Now the seconds you saved buy the part they can ask about.

Business

  1. 20 secondsUm, so I’m a business major, I’m taking marketing and accounting right now, and I kind of do the social media for my family’s restaurant, and I think it’s been going pretty well.All of that is there for you. None of it is for them.
  2. 12 secondsI’m a business major, and I do the social media for my family’s restaurant, and it’s been going pretty well.Same answer with the hedging and the course list gone.
  3. 8 secondsI do the social media for my family’s restaurant. I took Tuesday nights from twelve tables to forty.Now the seconds you saved buy the part they can ask about.
