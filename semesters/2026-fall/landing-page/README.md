# Landing page - Fall 2026

The club's public web page for this semester, live at <https://sam-t-g.github.io/rcc-gdg/>. The Club Rush table QR code points here, so the URL has to keep working after the fair. This folder is the source; GitHub Pages serves a build of it from the `gh-pages` branch.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | The page: an opening headline, then one pinned scroll scene where the semester's staircase draws itself while the Voice chapter works an example answer from 20 to 12 to 8 seconds, then Ask and Evidence, then the meeting time and the Discord button. The example comes in four majors, named inside the sentence ("You study nursing.") and cycled until the reader picks one. Styles come from `tokens.css`; rules it does not ship are marked PROPOSED |
| `favicon.svg` | The Step mark, green on light and light green on dark |
| `apple-touch-icon.png` | The Step on the page surface, 180 x 180, for home-screen bookmarks |
| `og.png` | The share card shown when the link is pasted into Discord or a bio: headline, meeting time, and the staircase, 1200 x 630, no logo ([components.md §8.6](../../../docs/04-brand/design-system/components.md)) |
| `qr-code.svg` | QR code for the page URL, error correction Q, four-module quiet zone. Scales to any print size |
| `qr-code-print.png` | The same code with the URL printed beneath, 900 x 1050 at 300 dpi: 3 in wide on paper. Not published to the site |

## Publish a change

From the repo root, on a branch whose changes to this folder are committed:

```sh
scripts/publish-site.sh 2026-fall          # build to site/ and stop; open site/index.html to check
scripts/publish-site.sh 2026-fall --push   # build and commit the result to gh-pages
```

GitHub Pages picks up the push within a minute or two. The build copies this folder, `docs/04-brand/design-system/tokens.css`, and the horizontal logo, and refuses to build if the independence sentence is missing or altered.

## Rules this page follows

- **The page has a large-display tier.** The type scale and the column both stop growing at 90rem, which leaves the page sitting small in the middle of a 4K screen, so above 1800px the column goes to 112rem and the display sizes carry on to their own caps. Above 1800px wide and 1100px tall the pinned scene is inset to the same column so the chapters do not sit against the screen edges while the opener stays centered.
- **The lockup is sized from the column, not from the viewport** (2026-09-20). On the old `vw` clamp it went from 69% of the measure on a phone to 24% on a laptop and never reached 45% of its own resolution, which is what read as small. Now it is `min(clamp(16.25rem, 40%, 43.75rem), 16svh * 4.9231)`: a flat 40% of the column at every width down to a 260px floor, with a height ceiling written as a width so a 390px-tall landscape phone does not hand 40% of a wide column to the logo. Only `inline-size` is ever set, with `block-size: auto`, which is the rule in [bright-lines.md 1.5](../../../docs/04-brand/design-system/bright-lines.md) that makes distortion impossible; a `max-block-size` ceiling was tried first and squashed the mark 1.58% on a landscape phone. The `4.9231` is the artwork's own 1920/390 and has to change with the file. At the 43.75rem cap and dpr 2 the lockup is 1400 device px against a 1920px source, so it never renders above native.
- **The opener is capped at 58rem, and the headline starts rather than ends.** `.intro` is a three-row grid whose middle row is `1fr`, so every spare pixel of viewport height goes into one gap. With `align-self: end` on the h1 that gap sat between the logo and the headline: 508px on an iPad portrait, 1000px at 900 x 1600, 47% and 62% of the opening screen. `align-self: start` keeps the logo and the headline as one block and drops the slack below them, and the cap stops a tall screen buying air by the screenful. The gap between the lockup and the headline is now 32px at every aspect ratio from 320 x 568 to 3840 x 2160.
- **The pinned scene sits in the page column at every width** (2026-09-20). `.wrap` is border-box at 90rem plus the two margins and centered, so its content edge is the larger of the page margin and half the leftover width. The chapters and the rail use that same expression, `max(var(--rcc-page-margin), calc((100% - 90rem) / 2))`, with 112rem in the large-display tier. Before this the inset was the raw margin, which matched the column only by coincidence at 1536px and below and left the chapters 72px to the left of the rest of the page at 1680 x 1050. The `100%` is the stage, which is the layout width with no scrollbar in it, so it is exact where `100vw` is off by the scrollbar.
- **The Voice column has to fit the pinned stage, so there are three short tiers.** The stage is 100svh and clips. The answer's reserved height is the tallest round of any major, so the size that fits is set by the longest sentence, not the one showing. Short laptops, 1024px wide and up to 840px tall (1280 x 720 ran 144px past the stage with the clock cut in half, 1366 x 768 and 1024 x 768 lost the note): the chapter inset, the rep rhythm, the answer and the clock all step down. Short phones, under 600px wide and up to 760px tall: as before. Any screen up to 500px tall, which is a phone on its side: it takes the short-phone tier plus the two-column Voice layout with the rep given seven parts in nine, the chapter line and the caption hidden, and the stair held to the bottom 16px band (`land` in `layout()`). At 844 x 390 the single-column rep had run 400px past the stage and the drawn stair crossed "You study".
- **Nothing on the page is measured by eye.** `scratch/club-rush-landing/render/` holds the check for this: an audit over 22 viewports from 320 x 568 to 3840 x 2160, eight scroll states each, that reports horizontal overflow, text clipped by the viewport, chapter content below the stage, text boxes that intersect, the rail touching a chapter name's ink, and the left edge of every block against the opener's. The rail's block box does intersect the chapter name's block box between 768 and 1024px wide; a Range rect on the name's text shows the ink never reaches the rail, which is what the `- 11rem` on the Voice chapter's width is for.
- **Light only.** The horizontal lockup exists only as a light file and the logo may not be recolored, so the page ignores a dark system theme rather than swapping in the stacked lockup.
- **One scroll scene carries the semester.** GSAP 3.15.0 and ScrollTrigger pin the stage and scrub one timeline: the Step staircase draws across the screen, the chapters hand off by opacity (so a screen reader still reads all three), and during Voice the example answer is worked in step with the scroll. Every state is computed from scroll position, so scrolling back rebuilds the sentence exactly.
- **The example is not a compression stunt.** Rounds 1 and 2 only delete: the filler, the hedges, the course list. Round 3 spends the time that freed up, so a specific detail and a real number arrive as the vague half goes. The end state has to be a sentence a person would actually say out loud, not a résumé fragment. Words leaving are struck through; words arriving come in green. Their windows overlap on purpose, because if the cut finished before the arrival started the line would sit for a beat as a stub with orphaned punctuation.
- **Three sentences per major are the only source.** The written rounds in the list are the input: the page word-diffs them at load and builds the animation from that, so the animated line cannot drift from the text a screen reader is given. Adding a major means writing three sentences, not hand-authoring a token list. Checked by `scratch/club-rush-landing/render/major-check.mjs`, which compares the animated line to the written round for every major at every clock.
- **The major is a word in the sentence.** "You study nursing." is the whole control: a green pill, because full round means you can press it (foundations §4), with a caret because that is the convention every select uses. A press rolls the word to the next major like an odometer and the pill resizes to fit. The sentence's height is reserved across all four majors so a longer name cannot add a line and shove the page down.
- **It cycles until the reader chooses.** Left alone it moves to the next major every 7 seconds, so a visitor sees within moments that the page is not about one major. That motion outlasts 5 seconds, so WCAG 2.2.2 requires a way to stop it and the Pause button beside the hint is it. Following the [APG carousel pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/carousel/) it also holds under the pointer, while keyboard focus is inside, while the reader is scrolling, and while another chapter is showing; it stops for good the moment anyone picks a major, and reduced motion never starts it. The hint row keeps its height when the Pause button goes, so stopping the cycle does not move the page. The cycle runs on `gsap.delayedCall`, which rides GSAP's rAF ticker, so a background tab freezes it with no visibility bookkeeping.
- **Automatic swaps are silent for screen readers.** The cycle checks the same radios a press does, so the written rounds a screen reader gets always match what is on screen, but only a chosen major is announced through the status region. Announcing every 7-second swap would interrupt a reader with something they did not ask for.
- **The picker works without scripts.** Four radios drive the written rounds through `:has()`, so reduced motion and a blocked CDN still get a working picker; without `:has()` all four sets show, each under its own heading. Without scripts the pill and the cycle are not there at all and the four names below do the picking.
- **The example answer is labelled as an example.** Its three rounds are also written out in a list that screen readers read and that shows on its own for reduced motion or when scripts fail.
- **GSAP loads from cdnjs**, pinned, with the SRI hashes cdnjs publishes, under the [GSAP standard license](https://gsap.com/standard-license), not vendored into this repo. Without it, or with reduced motion, the page is a static stack of the same content.
- **The independence sentence appears twice:** at the bottom of the opening screen, because the page runs past three screens with the logo above the fold, and in the footer ([bright-lines.md §1.4](../../../docs/04-brand/design-system/bright-lines.md)).
- No officer names and no photos of people ([privacy-and-public-repo-policy.md](../../../docs/01-governance/privacy-and-public-repo-policy.md)). No icons and no callouts.
- **The cycle stops for good once the reader picks, in either order.** `stopCycle` latches `started`, not just `cycling`. A reader can tab to the pill and press it before the first scroll-idle has ever started the cycle, and without the latch that idle would then start cycling over a choice already made.
- **Every control is checked with real input.** `scratch/club-rush-landing/render/input-check.mjs` presses through the debugger protocol (touch on phones, mouse on desktop) and first asks `elementFromPoint` what is under the press, because the earlier harness called `.click()` from script and passed a picker that no finger could reach: the three stacked chapters sit in one spot and the transparent ones were swallowing every tap. `rotate-check.mjs` holds the cycle to each of the rules above.
