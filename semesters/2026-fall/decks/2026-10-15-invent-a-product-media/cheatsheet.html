Invent a product

No code. Pick a real problem, find out why it happens, and pitch a fix.

  1. 1Problem
  2. 2Why
  3. 3Ideas
  4. 4Build
  5. 5Checks
  6. 6Pitch
1

5 minutes · in your group

What annoyed you this week?

  • Everyone names one thing.
  • Pick one you’ve felt yourself.
  • Stuck? Try parking, scalpers, textbook codes, or getting into a full class.
  • CheckWho do you know who has it?
2

8 minutes · in your group

Why does it happen?

  • Who has it? Be exact.
  • When is it worst?
  • What do they do about it now?
  • Why hasn’t anyone fixed it?

___ can’t ___ because ___.Students with 9:00 classes can’t find a spot because they can’t see which ones are open.

3

8 minutes · on your own

Fill all eight boxes.

  • Crazy 8s. Fold a sheet into eight boxes, one idea per box. No talking yet.
  • Box 3: How would an airline do it?
  • Box 4: Who’d fix this for money?
  • Box 5: Fix it with a new rule.
  • Box 7: Your weirdest idea.
4

6 minutes · in your group

Pick one and build it out.

  • Share your best two, then vote.
  • Give it a name.
  • How does it work? Three steps.
  • Who pays for it?
  • CheckLook it up. Does it exist?
5

Challenge checks · 5 minutes

What would actually happen?

  • Who are your first 10 users?
  • It costs money every month. Who pays?
  • The school says no. What’s plan B?
  • A big company copies you. Now what?
  • Would people trust it with their info?

Pick the one that hurts your idea most, and change your idea to handle it.

6

5 minutes · 60-second pitch

Plan your pitch with STAR

  • SSituation: Who has the problem, and when?
  • TTask: What has to change? Your because.
  • AAction: What you built, in three steps.
  • RResult: What gets better, and who pays.

Then one challenge you fixed. Spend most of the time on the A.

Before next Thursday

Ask one person who has your problem if they’d use your idea.

Bring back what they said, even if it’s no.
