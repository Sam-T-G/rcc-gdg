Paid summer research, open to community college students

Research

Start at RCC

  • RCC STEM Connect2027 [TBD]

    Paid summer research with professors at UCR, CSUSB, CSU Fullerton, and RCC. 114 RCC students in 2025, stipends $500 to $4,000. Ask your STEM professors or the STEM Engagement Center (MLK 306) when it opens.

  • RCC-UCR Bridges to the BaccalaureateRolling

    Research in UCR labs while you’re at RCC. 3.0 GPA, two faculty letters, and an interview.

National programs

  • DOE Community College Internships (CCI)Jan 6

    Only for community college students. 10 weeks at one of the DOE national labs, $650 a week plus housing help. 2.7 GPA, US citizen or permanent resident.

  • DOE SULIJan 6

    Same labs and stipend. Full-time students at a community college or university, 3.0 GPA.

  • NIH Summer Internship ProgramJan 26

    Open to students at least half-time at a community college. Science fields, including psychology, nursing, and social science. About $3,000 a month; no housing.

  • NSF REU sites (search all of them)Jan to Mar

    Paid summer research at a university, about $700 a week, often with housing. NSF asks every site to fill half its spots from schools with less research, including two-year colleges.

  • Cal Poly Pomona CAMP REUFeb 15

    $7,000 summer research, close to Riverside. Needs Calculus 3.

  • UCSD STARSSummer 2027

    Summer research at UC San Diego, June 20 to August 13, 2027. Community college students in any field; AB 540 students can apply.

  • Cal-Bridge FAST and Summer ProgramFeb 1

    Paid summer research for California community college students in physics, astronomy, CS, engineering, and math. Housing and travel covered for the Summer Program.

  • NASA Community College Aerospace Scholars2027 [TBD]

    Online challenge for community college students: webinars with NASA staff, then a project. Finishers get $500.

Show your work

  • Honors Transfer Council research conferenceSpring 2027

    Community college honors students present their research at a UC campus.

  • NCUR 2027Dec 4

    National conference for undergrad research. Two-year college students welcome.

No program? Email a professor

  1. Go to office hours with an RCC professor. Ask about STEM Connect, B2B, and who they know at UCR.
  2. Find 5 to 10 professors at UCR or CSUSB whose lab pages match what you want to learn.
  3. Email from your school address. Three short paragraphs: who you are, why their lab, and when you’re free. Attach your resume.
  4. Follow up once.

Jobs and internships

On campus

  • Tutor at RCCRolling

    $17.00 to $17.75 an hour (advanced $19.00 to $19.75). An A or B in the subject and a 3.0 GPA.

  • RCC student jobsRolling

    Campus jobs up to 20 hours a week, $16.90 to $20.75 an hour in 2026-27. Postings are on the district job portal. Federal Work-Study needs a FAFSA.

  • College Corps at RCCInterest list

    A paid year of community service, 450 hours, up to $7,000 plus a $3,000 award. Full-time RCC students. This year’s round is closed; join the interest list.

Riverside and nearby

  • City of Riverside internshipsRolling

    Paid and unpaid internships in city departments for college students.

  • County of Riverside internshipsRolling

    Unpaid spots are open to any college student. Paid spots need a bachelor’s program.

  • Inland Empire Utilities AgencyRolling

    Paid internship at a regional water agency, $21 an hour.

Federal

  • Federal Pathways internshipsRolling

    Paid internships at federal agencies, open to community college students. Posted on USAJOBS.

  • NASA internshipsMar 1

    Paid, STEM and non-STEM majors. 3.0 GPA, US citizen.

Moves that make a transfer application stronger

Transfer

UC

  • UC Transfer Admission Planner (UC TAP)Semester 1

    Track your classes for the UCs. You file TAG here, and it’s step 1 for UCR R-STEP and UCLA CCCP.

  • UC Transfer Admission Guarantee (TAG)Sept 1 to 30

    Guaranteed spot at one of Davis, Irvine, Merced, Riverside, Santa Barbara, or Santa Cruz. Some majors are left out: for fall 2027, Davis left out CS and Irvine left out all of its computer science school; UCR takes CS with a 3.6. Starting fall 2028, Irvine drops TAG for several engineering majors, biology, economics, and psychology. Check the TAG matrix.

  • UC Pathways+Free

    Take one of 27 UC Transfer Pathway majors’ classes and add a TAG for a guaranteed spot.

  • UCR Road to RiversideRolling

    Only for RCCD students headed to UCR: a UCR counselor twice a year and the option to take UCR classes.

  • UCR R-STEPFree

    Free one-on-one transfer help from UCR admissions for any community college student.

  • UCLA CCCP ScholarsMay to June, last year

    A summer program at UCLA for community college students with a 3.0 UC GPA.

  • UC Berkeley Community College Transfer ServicesOpen now

    Transfer prep for any California community college student, for any UC.

At RCC

  • RCC Honors and UCLA TAPFall or spring

    3.0 GPA. Priority review at UCLA (not a guarantee) for UCLA College majors. Engineering (including CS), Arts, Music, Nursing, and Theater/Film don’t take part.

  • EOPSBefore 45 units

    Priority registration, book vouchers, counseling. Only students under 45 units can join.

  • MESARolling

    STEM counseling, tutoring, and research and internship help for first-generation students in calculus-based majors.

  • Puente and UmojaRolling

    A year with the same counselor and mentors, built around transferring.

  • RCC Transfer CenterCAK 101

    Transfer counseling, the Transfer Fair in September, and TAG help.

CSU

  • Associate Degree for Transfer (ADT)Jan 1 to Feb 15

    Priority admission at the CSUs for a similar major. Apply for the ADT in MyPortal by Feb 15 for fall transfer.

  • HBCU Transfer GuaranteeRolling

    Guaranteed admission to 30+ HBCUs with a 2.0 GPA and an ADT or 30 UC/CSU units.

What to do when

Your four semesters

Semester 1
  • Make a UC TAP account
  • Join EOPS, MESA, Puente, or Umoja if you qualify
  • Start RCC Honors if it fits your major
  • Set up Handshake
  • First term: CSU Transfer Success Pathway
Semester 2 and summer
  • Apply for summer research and internships, December to March
  • Tutor or take a campus job
  • RCCD Foundation scholarships in January
  • Go to a hackathon
  • Reach 30 UC units by summer for a TAG
Semester 3
  • TAG in September
  • UC and CSU applications, October 1 to November 30
  • Jack Kent Cooke by early December
Semester 4
  • UC Transfer Academic Update in January
  • ADT petition by February 15
  • FAFSA by March 2
  • Decisions March to May
