### Why it is paced this way

`LEAD` runs 7 of 14, which is deliberate rather than a failure to delegate. The lead takes the four kinds of room that punish inexperience:

- **The two hook sessions.** They are the club's recruiting, and half the room is strangers deciding whether to return.
- **The two transfer weeks that go personal** (Oct 1, Oct 29). Someone will say something heavier than expected, and Oct 29 has the redirect for genuinely serious situations. That is not a first-timer's room.
- **Both receipt weeks** (Oct 8, Nov 5). They have a hard external dependency (wi-fi) and a real failure mode (people draft and never send).
- **Demo Day.** Guests, a timer, and the semester's numbers.

Rotators get install and pressure weeks, which is where the mechanics are already on a slide and the worst outcome is a phase running long. `FAC-D` carries the lightest load on purpose: give that seat to the newest officer, and it is the seat that grows into a heavier one in spring.

Nobody runs two weeks in a row. Every rotator backs up at least twice before they run anything, so their first time in front of the room is not their first time holding the card.

### If you have fewer people

The scheme collapses without editing any card. Just point two seats at one person in the table above:

- **Three facilitators:** `FAC-C` and `FAC-D` both go to the person holding `FAC-A` and `FAC-B` respectively.
- **Two facilitators:** `FAC-A` and `FAC-C` to one person, `FAC-B` and `FAC-D` to the other.
- **One facilitator:** everything is `LEAD`. The cards still run; the club just loses the rotation, and that goes in [retrospective.md](../retrospective.md) as the thing to fix in spring.

`FLOAT-1`, `FLOAT-2`, and `TIME` are jobs rather than people. Anyone in the room who is not running it can take one, including a member who is not an officer.

### Putting real names in

Two options, and the first is usually right:

1. **Fill the Person column above and leave the cards alone.** Seats stay stable across semesters, so next year's team inherits the rotation shape without inheriting this year's names.
2. **Bake a name into the cards** if a facilitator wants their own name on their script: `sed -i '' 's/`FAC-A`/Name/g' semesters/2026-fall/sessions/*.md`. Only do this with written consent on file, and note that it makes the cards single-semester rather than reusable.

Log any mid-semester swap in [meetings/](../meetings/README.md) for that week. A swap is not a binding decision and does not need a decision-log entry.

## The three arcs

**Voice** (Sep 17 to Oct 8). Be understood by someone who has no reason to listen to you. Most students can describe their work to a friend and not to a stranger, and the stranger is the one who decides things.

**Ask** (Oct 15 to Nov 5). Request things from people with power, and use a no. The single highest-leverage unlearned skill at a community college: nobody tells students that the transfer letter, the research spot, and the schedule exception are all available to whoever asks well.

**Evidence** (Nov 12 to Dec 10). Make your work visible, defensible, and legible to someone who was not there. Students routinely finish a semester of real work with nothing another person can verify.

## Why the first two are different

Club Rush runs September 15 and 16, and official programming starts the next day. September 3 and September 10 are hook sessions: no arc, no continuity, built for a room of strangers where half will never come back and the other half decide that day whether they will. They are front-loaded on purpose, and September 10 exists specifically to arm members for tabling the following week.

## Not in this folder

Anything technical. The Modular Impact Sprint and DevFest run as separate published events with their own folders under [events/](../events/README.md), announced from the weekly meeting as the next rung. The weekly meeting is never a coding session; that is what makes it work for a room where most people do not code.

## Running one

1. Read the card and [meeting-algorithm.md](../../../docs/03-playbooks/meeting-algorithm.md).
2. `scripts/new-meeting.sh 2026-fall YYYY-MM-DD` for the notes file.
3. Run the block. Move the clock.
4. Record the attendance count and the receipt count in the notes within 48 hours.
5. If a phase failed, edit the card here. The card is the maintained copy.
