The problem this club exists for

Debrief

Hands up: who found that easier than expected?

Keep them up. Now, who thinks it would have been harder if the card were yours?

3:15, three minutes. Two or three answers each, not more. Then: what exactly was different? Push past "I was less nervous" to the real answer: nothing was at stake for you personally, so you asked plainly. Plainness is the whole skill. Then: somebody in this room is holding your card. What do you want them to do with it?
