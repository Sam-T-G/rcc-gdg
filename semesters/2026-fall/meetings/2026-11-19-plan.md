## Research

Talking points:

- Some research programs take only CC students. NSF tells REU host sites to recruit at least half their students from schools with limited research, "including two-year colleges."
- **Start at RCC.** RCC STEM Connect placed 114 RCC students in paid summer labs at UCR, CSUSB, CSU Fullerton, and RCC in 2025 ($500 to $4,000). RCC-UCR Bridges to the Baccalaureate (NIH-funded) puts RCC students in UCR labs; it asks for a 3.0 GPA and two faculty letters. [TBD: STEM Connect's program page is down; confirm it runs in 2027.]
- **NSF REU:** paid summer research (about $700 a week, often with housing), applied for site by site, mostly due January to March. Search: <https://www.nsf.gov/funding/initiatives/reu/search>. Examples that want CC students: Cal Poly Pomona CAMP, UCSC Lamat, Arizona C2C (geoscience, CC students only).
- **UCSD STARS:** summer research open to CC students in all fields, AB 540 students included. Jun 20 to Aug 13, 2027.
- **National labs and agencies:** DOE CCI and SULI, NIH SIP, NASA (dates above). NASA Community College Aerospace Scholars is an online challenge for CC students with a $500 stipend for finishers [TBD: 2027 dates].
- **Present your work:** HTCC honors research conference (spring 2027, at a UC campus, for CC honors students) and NCUR.
- **No program? Email a professor.** From UCR's, UCLA's, and UT Austin's own guides: go to office hours with an RCC professor first, then list 5 to 10 professors at UCR or CSUSB whose lab pages match your interest. Use your school email, keep it to three short paragraphs (who you are, why their lab, when you're free), attach a resume, and follow up once.

## Jobs and internships

Talking points:

- **On campus at RCC** (2026-27 pay scale $16.90 to $20.75 an hour): tutor ($17.00 to $17.75; needs an A or B in the subject and a 3.0 GPA), STEM Mentor ($19.00 to $19.75), peer instructor in the Math Learning Center or Writing and Reading Center. Postings are on the district job portal. Federal Work-Study needs a FAFSA.
- **College Corps at RCC:** a paid year of community service, 450 hours, up to $7,000 stipend plus a $3,000 award. Full-time RCC students. The 2026-27 round is closed; RCC has an interest list for the next one.
- **Local:** City of Riverside internships (paid and unpaid, open to college students), County of Riverside (unpaid spots open to any college student; paid spots need a bachelor's program), Inland Empire Utilities Agency ($21 an hour, college students).
- **Federal:** OPM's Pathways internships name community colleges as eligible. They post on USAJOBS.
- **Big tech:** Microsoft Explore, Amazon, Capital One, and Salesforce all say "bachelor's" in their eligibility. Google STEP had no postings on 2026-11-18. The CC programs above take you now; big tech opens up after transfer.
- **Where to look:** Handshake (free for RCC students, log in with your RCCD email), RCC Career Center in Kane 101 (resume help, mock interviews, two free outfits a semester from the Career Closet).
- **Units for a job you already have:** Work Experience (WKX) courses give credit for paid or volunteer work [TBD: confirm hours per unit on RCC's catalog page, which didn't load].

## Transfer moves

Talking points:

- **Make a UC TAP account in semester 1.** It tracks your courses, it's where TAG gets filed, and it's step 1 for UCR R-STEP and UCLA CCCP.
- **UC TAG:** guaranteed admission at one of six UCs (Davis, Irvine, Merced, Riverside, Santa Barbara, Santa Cruz), filed Sept 1 to 30 the year before you transfer. For fall 2027, CS was excluded at Davis and all of UCI's computer science school was excluded. UCR keeps CS open with a 3.6 GPA. Starting fall 2028, UCI drops TAG for computer, electrical, mechanical, and aerospace engineering, biology, economics, psychology, and more. That hits people who are first-years now.
- **Pathways+:** take a UC Transfer Pathway major's courses and add a TAG for a guaranteed spot.
- **UCR for RCC students:** Road to Riverside (RCCD students only; UCR counselor twice a year, option to take UCR classes) and R-STEP (free advising for any CC student).
- **RCC Honors and UCLA TAP:** 3.0 GPA (Sam confirmed). Priority review at UCLA, not a guarantee, and only for UCLA College majors plus a few schools. Engineering (including CS), Arts, Music, Nursing, and Theater/Film don't take part. Starting May 2026, one-year transfers can't be TAP-certified.
- **Join early:** EOPS only takes students under 45 units. MESA is for first-generation students in calculus-based majors. Puente and Umoja support transfer for a full year.
- **CSU:** an Associate Degree for Transfer gets priority at the CSUs; apply for it in MyPortal Jan 1 to Feb 15 for fall transfer.
- **Summer programs at UCs:** UCLA CCCP Scholars (summer at UCLA, 3.0 UC GPA; last cycle due May to June) and Berkeley's Community College Transfer Services (any CA community college).
- **Scholarships:** Jack Kent Cooke (Dec 9), RCCD Foundation (Jan 17), Cal-HBCU Transfer Grant (up to $5,000).
- **Hackathons:** Hacktech at Caltech (Feb 26 to 28, 2027; says CC students are welcome, free), LA Hacks (mid-April 2027), and UCR's Citrus Hack and Cutie Hack. A built project and a team role give you something real to write about in the UC Personal Insight Questions.
- **Private schools, if anyone asks:** Caltech Feb 1, USC Feb 15, Stanford and Harvey Mudd Mar 15 (2027 transfer deadlines).

## The four-semester timeline

| Semester | Do this |
|---|---|
| 1 | UC TAP account. Join EOPS, MESA, Puente, or Umoja if you qualify. Start RCC Honors if it fits your major. Handshake and Career Center. Sign the CSU Transfer Success Pathway if you're in your first term. |
| 2 | Apply for summer research and internships (Dec to Mar). Campus job or tutoring once you have the grades. RCCD Foundation scholarships by mid-January. A hackathon. |
| Summer | Research or an internship. Reach 30 UC units if you want a TAG. |
| 3 | TAG in September. UC and CSU applications Oct 1 to Nov 30. Jack Kent Cooke by early December. |
| 4 | Transfer Academic Update, ADT petition, FAFSA by early March. Decisions March to May. |
