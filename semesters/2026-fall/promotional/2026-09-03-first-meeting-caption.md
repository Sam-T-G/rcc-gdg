Our first meeting of the year is Thursday, September 3, 2:30 to 3:30 PM in BLCIS A-210.

Session one is The Cold Open. Everyone writes down one thing they want this year that they cannot get by themselves. The cards get shuffled and dealt back out, and you spend the hour asking the room for something on a stranger's behalf. No names go on the cards, and every card is destroyed in the room before you leave.

It turns out to be much easier to advocate for a stranger than for yourself. Noticing that about yourself is the whole point, and it takes about forty minutes.

Who should come: any major, any year.
Experience needed: none.
Bring: nothing. We have the cards and the pens.
RSVP: none. Walk in.

GDG on Campus @ RCC meets Thursdays, 2:30 to 3:30 PM in BLCIS A-210 Simulation Lab.

GDG on Campus Riverside City College is an independent group; our activities and the opinions expressed here should in no way be linked to Google, the corporation.

\#GDGOnCampus
