What happens in the room

Each Thursday the room practices one skill out loud for an hour: the sentence that gets a stranger to ask you a follow-up question, holding your ground when the listener is not interested, asking for the thing you actually want. Every session works as your first one, so walk in any week.

The semester

Three arcs, each ending with something you did for real.

  1. Voice Now

    September 17 to October 8

    Ends with a real message you send from the room

  2. Ask Now

    October 15 to November 5

    Ends with an appointment you book with someone who has power

  3. Evidence Now

    November 12 to December 10

    Ends at Demo Day: three minutes in front of outsiders

If you write code

Every session ends by naming the technical version of the skill. Saying what you do in twenty seconds is the first paragraph of a README; asking a question someone can act on is a bug report. Technical events run separately and are announced in the Discord.
