# Playbook: Silicon Valley Trip

## Purpose

A multi-day trip for about ten members to the Bay Area: the Google Visitor Experience in Mountain View, plus campus visits at Stanford and San Jose State. Two goals: put members in front of the places they are transferring toward, and put the club name in front of Google. This is the "Trips (College & Google Tours)" line in the FY 2026-27 ASRCC funding request.

Trips are the hardest thing the club does. ASRCC's funding priority is on-campus engagement, the advisor has to travel with the group, and every dollar has to be reimbursed against a receipt. Read the whole playbook before promising anyone a seat.

## Budget shape (from the FY 2026-27 request)

Line item: "Trips (College & Google Tours)". Destinations: Googleplex visitor experience, Stanford, SJSU. About 10 students.

| Sub-item | Requested | Per student (10 students) | Covers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Travel | $3,500 | $350 | Flights or long-distance ground transport to the Bay Area |
| Lodging | $1,400 | $140 | Shared rooms, 2 nights |
| Transportation | $1,100 | $110 | Local transit, rideshare, parking, rental |
| Other sub-items | "plus more" per the request; figures [TBD] | | Meals, admission, contingency |
| Listed subtotal | $6,000 | $600 | |

The whole request is $12,000 across all lines. Whether ASRCC funded this line, in full or in part: [TBD]. The per-student column is arithmetic on the requested figures; it becomes real only after allocation.

Sanity check on the travel line: ACM @ RCC priced ONT to SFO flights at $180 per person for Cal Hacks 12.0 in fall 2025, with about $320 total ground transit for 11 people and $110.99 per person per night for a shared AirBnB. Those numbers are in the ACM repo at https://github.com/Sam-T-G/rcc-acm. Against them, $350 per student for travel is comfortable, $140 per student for two nights of lodging is tight, and $110 per student for local transit is generous if the group moves together.

## Funding rules that bite

- ASRCC funds from the Student Services Fee Fund; priority goes to on-campus student engagement, so expect questions at the hearing about what the trip does for members who stay home. Have an answer: a recap meeting, photos (with consent), and a transfer-info session run by the travelers.
- ASRCC does not fund fundraisers, personnel, or things the college must provide.
- Acknowledge ASRCC in any post or recap about the trip.
- Reimbursement needs receipts with price and date visible. Screenshots of a booking confirmation without a price do not count ([reimbursements.md](../02-operations/reimbursements.md)).
- Budget and Trust Requisition forms go through Student Activities; the advisor signs. Details in [asrcc-funding.md](../02-operations/asrcc-funding.md).
- Members cover the gap between allocation and cost. State the per-person contribution before anyone commits.

## Trip run sheet (3 days, 2 nights)

| Day | Block | What |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | morning | Depart Riverside; head count at every transfer |
| 1 | afternoon | Check in; short orientation walk; group dinner |
| 2 | morning | Google Visitor Experience, Mountain View |
| 2 | afternoon | Stanford campus tour and transfer session |
| 2 | evening | Debrief: one thing each person learned; photos with consent |
| 3 | morning | SJSU campus tour and transfer session |
| 3 | afternoon | Return; receipts collected on the way home |

Order the campuses by whichever tour times you get. If a Google meetup is offered, it takes priority over everything else on that day.

## Rules on the road

- Nobody leaves the group without telling the trip lead; buddy pairs at all times.
- The advisor holds the emergency contacts and makes any call about a medical or safety issue.
- The code of conduct applies for the whole trip, hotel included.
- Every receipt goes in the envelope the treasurer hands out on day 1, with the payer's name on it.
