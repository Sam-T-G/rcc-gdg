# Playbook: DevFest

## Purpose

DevFest is Google's global, community-led tech conference series, hosted by Google Developer Groups. Google's page calls it the largest community-driven tech conference in the world, aimed at developers and AI builders, across AI, Cloud, Mobile, and Web. Agendas are set locally by each chapter. For RCC, DevFest is the fall anchor event: the one day a semester where the club runs several sessions back to back, brings in outside speakers, and aims for the 50+ attendance the FY 2026-27 funding request promised.

Two ways to take part. Pick one each fall and log it in the [decision log](../01-governance/decision-log.md):

| Mode | What it means | Good when |
|---|---|---|
| Attend | Take a group to a nearby GDG chapter's DevFest | First year, small team, no budget yet |
| Host | Run "DevFest Riverside City College" on campus, listed on Bevy | Two or more organizers free to run it; room and speakers lined up by early October |

Whether GDG on Campus chapters may list their own DevFest under the program banner is not stated on Google's DevFest pages. Confirm with the GDG program contact before publishing a hosted event with "DevFest" in the title: [TBD]. Nearest GDG chapter DevFest to attend: [TBD].

## When

The DevFest season runs October through December (Google publishes the exact window each year on the DevFest page). Fall academic dates (first day, holidays, the November break, finals) are in this semester's `calendar.md`; for Fall 2026 see [semesters/2026-fall/calendar.md](../../semesters/2026-fall/calendar.md).

## Owner role

| Role | Does |
|---|---|
| Event lead (one organizer) | Dates, room block, Bevy event, speaker lineup, run of show |
| Speaker wrangler | Invites, confirms, and hosts each speaker ([guest-speaker.md](guest-speaker.md)) |
| Workshop lead | Runs the hands-on block ([workshop.md](workshop.md) or [workshop-modular-impact-sprint.md](workshop-modular-impact-sprint.md)) |
| Tech runner | Projector, audio, stream, adapters, backup laptop |
| Check-in desk (two members) | Bevy Organizer App, name tags, ASRCC sign |
| Advisor | Attends the whole event; required for on-campus club events |

## Hosted format (half day, about 3.5 hours)

| Block | Length | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Doors and check-in | 30 min | Bevy check-in, name tags, coffee |
| Opening | 10 min | What GDG on Campus is, thanks to ASRCC and the advisor, the day's plan |
| Talk 1 | 30 min | Outside speaker (Google, industry, or transfer-university faculty) |
| Talk 2 | 30 min | Second speaker, or a student team demo |
| Break | 15 min | |
| Hands-on workshop | 60 min | The Modular Impact Sprint or a codelab tied to the season theme |
| Lightning talks | 20 min | Members, 5 minutes each |
| Close | 10 min | Next meeting date, Bevy and Discord links, photos with consenting attendees |

One room with a projector is enough. A second room for the workshop helps if attendance passes 40.

## T-minus checklist (host mode)

- [ ] T-10 weeks (early September): mode decision logged. Confirm the "DevFest" title question with the program contact.
- [ ] T-10 weeks: advisor requests the room block in 25Live, well over the two-week minimum, with tables, chairs, and projector in the original request ([room-booking.md](../02-operations/room-booking.md)). BLCIS is the default building.
- [ ] T-9 weeks: Student Activities Event Form; Food Authorization form; notify College Safety and Police as the Advisor's Guide requires ([event-checklist.md](../02-operations/event-checklist.md)).
- [ ] T-8 weeks: invite speakers. Two confirmed speakers by T-6. Use the guest speaker playbook for travel, lodging, and thank-you handling.
- [ ] T-6 weeks: publish on Bevy first ([bevy-event-publishing.md](bevy-event-publishing.md)); then Discord, Instagram, and classroom announcements ([communications.md](../02-operations/communications.md)). Ask faculty in CIS and engineering to announce it in class.
- [ ] T-6 weeks: create the event folder with `scripts/new-event.sh`; fill in the [plan](../../templates/event-plan.md) and the [funding line item](../../templates/funding-line-item.md) if spending against the ASRCC request.
- [ ] T-4 weeks: workshop dry run with the workshop lead. Confirm Wi-Fi handles 50 laptops in that room or plan for phone hotspots.
- [ ] T-3 weeks: second announcement wave; ask sibling clubs (ACM @ RCC is the obvious one) to cross-post.
- [ ] T-2 weeks: speaker tech check by video call; collect slides in the shared drive.
- [ ] T-1 week: [run sheet](../../templates/event-run-sheet.md) final; print signage, ASRCC acknowledgement, name tags, feedback QR.
- [ ] T-2 days: order food to the checked-in average plus a margin, not the RSVP count. Bevy's dashboard shows registrations by day, so watch the curve.
- [ ] T-1 day: room walk-through with the tech runner.

## T-minus checklist (attend mode)

- [ ] T-6 weeks: pick the chapter DevFest; confirm it is free or note the ticket price [TBD].
- [ ] T-5 weeks: post the Bevy link for that event in Discord; members register themselves.
- [ ] T-4 weeks: transportation plan (carpool or campus vehicle per RCC travel rules [TBD]); advisor attendance confirmed.
- [ ] T-1 week: head count; group chat; meeting point and time.
- [ ] After: same retro steps as host mode; attendance is the count of members who checked in at that chapter's event.

## After

- [ ] Export registered vs checked-in from Bevy the same week; counts in `semesters/<term>/retrospective.md`; this is the number that goes into next year's ASRCC request
- [ ] `retro.md` in the event folder within two weeks
- [ ] Thank-you to each speaker within 48 hours; log speaker organization and topic (names only with consent) in the event folder
- [ ] Recording to the club shared drive; Bevy recordings are available to members
- [ ] Recap post with #GDGOnCampus
- [ ] Receipts to the treasurer ([reimbursements.md](../02-operations/reimbursements.md))
- [ ] Decision log entry: attend or host next year, based on the checked-in count

## Budget shape

DevFest is the natural home for the FY 2026-27 "Google Guest Speakers & Workshops" line: $2,000 for the year, 50+ attendance, held in BLCIS. Sub-items from the request:

| Sub-item | Annual figure | DevFest use |
|---|---|---|
| Transportation | $500 | Speaker travel (mileage or a train ticket) |
| Lodging | $500 | One night for an out-of-area speaker |
| Meals | $600 | Attendee food; speaker lunch |
| Marketing / swag | $200 | Signage, name tags, small prizes |
| Technology | $100 | Adapters, a cheap microphone |
| Supplies | $100 | Printing, tape, markers |

These are annual totals shared with every other workshop; decide the DevFest share in the decision log. FY 2026-27 allocation status: [TBD]. ASRCC does not fund fundraisers or personnel and requires acknowledgement at funded events ([asrcc-funding.md](../02-operations/asrcc-funding.md)). Speakers cannot be paid from the request; a thank-you gift under the swag line is fine.
