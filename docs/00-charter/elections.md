## Who can run

- Current RCC student and a member under [membership.md](membership.md).
- Able to serve the whole term. Officers who transfer mid-term create a vacancy; see below.
- For President and any Bevy organizer role: willing to have their name and title listed publicly on the chapter page, since Google publishes organizers on gdg.community.dev.
- Google may set its own eligibility rules for organizers through its application (<https://developers.google.com/community/gdg>, "Lead a GDG on Campus"). Check the current rules before nominating. [TBD: record the current rules and their date in the semester folder]
- ICC Representative: cannot represent another club at the same time (ICC By-Laws).

## Process

1. **Announce.** At least two weekly meetings before the vote, announce the election date, open roles, and how to run. Post it on Discord and in the Bevy event description for that meeting.
2. **Nominate.** Self-nominations and nominations by others. Candidates confirm they accept. Nominations close [TBD: at the meeting before the vote, or at the start of the election meeting].
3. **Speak.** Each candidate gets a short statement at the election meeting (two minutes is enough).
4. **Vote.** [TBD: method]. Two common options: paper ballot counted by the advisor, or an anonymous form counted by two officers who are not running. The count is announced at the meeting.
5. **Tie.** [TBD: runoff at the same meeting, or advisor casts the deciding vote].
6. **Record.** Write one paragraph in the [decision log](../01-governance/decision-log.md): date, roles filled, method, turnout (a number, not names). Names of winners appear only with consent.

## After the election, within one week

| Step | Owner |
|---|---|
| Update the ASRCC club registration form with the new officers | Outgoing President with advisor |
| Update organizers on the Bevy chapter dashboard (add new, remove outgoing) | Outgoing President |
| Update [MAINTAINERS.md](../../MAINTAINERS.md); keep two admins at all times | Outgoing repo admin |
| Transfer Discord and Instagram ownership or admin rights | Outgoing Secretary |
| Update the trust account signers with Student Activities | Outgoing Treasurer with advisor |
| Start `handoff.md` in the semester folder | Every outgoing officer |

The full handoff sequence is in [handoff-procedure.md](../01-governance/handoff-procedure.md).
