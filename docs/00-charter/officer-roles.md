# Officer roles

Who holds each role right now is in the current semester's [roster.md](../../semesters/2026-fall/roster.md) (counts and roles) and [MAINTAINERS.md](../../MAINTAINERS.md) (GitHub handles). Names appear only with the officer's consent.

RCC requires a President, a Treasurer, and an ICC Representative, plus one full-time faculty advisor (<https://www.rcc.edu/life-at-rcc/clubs-and-organizations.html>). Google describes the organizer role as "Plan and host meetup events on a wide range of technical topics typically on a monthly basis" (<https://developers.google.com/profile/badges/community/gdg/GDGoC/organizer>). Everything else below is the club's own division of labor.

## Roles

| Role | Required by | Responsibilities | Owns in this repo |
|---|---|---|---|
| President (lead organizer) | RCC; Google (organizer of record) | Runs officer meetings; sets semester goals; primary contact for Student Activities, Google, and this repo; final call on event go/no-go; signs off on the semester close | `README.md`, `docs/00-charter/`, `docs/01-governance/`, `semesters/<term>/goals.md`, `retrospective.md`, `handoff.md` |
| Vice President | Club | Steps in for the President; owns the event pipeline from proposal to retro; runs Demo Day and the Solutions Challenge track | `docs/03-playbooks/`, `semesters/<term>/events/`, `semesters/<term>/projects/` |
| Secretary | Club | Meeting notes; attendance and roster counts; announcements; keeps Discord and Instagram current | `semesters/<term>/meetings/`, `semesters/<term>/roster.md`, `docs/02-operations/attendance-tracking.md`, `docs/02-operations/communications.md`, `templates/announcement.md` |
| Treasurer | RCC | Semester budget; ASRCC funding request each spring; requisitions and reimbursements with the advisor; ASRCC acknowledgment at funded events | `semesters/<term>/budget.md`, `docs/02-operations/asrcc-funding.md`, `docs/02-operations/reimbursements.md`, `templates/funding-line-item.md` |
| ICC Representative | RCC | Attends ICC (Mondays 9:30 AM, Bradshaw Center Hall of Fame, per the ASRCC page); stays under three absences per semester; brings ICC deadlines back to the officers; carries the recharter packet | `docs/02-operations/club-recognition-and-icc.md`, ICC rows in `semesters/<term>/calendar.md` |
| Organizer (Bevy) | Google | Any officer with organizer access on gdg.community.dev; publishes events, runs check-in, exports attendance reports | `docs/03-playbooks/bevy-event-publishing.md`, the event folder for events they run |
| Faculty Advisor | RCC | Submits 25Live room requests (students cannot); attends events; signs requisitions; fallback contact for accounts and this repo | Reviewer on `docs/02-operations/room-booking.md`; listed in `MAINTAINERS.md` |
| Repo admin | This repo | At least two at all times, one not graduating this year; merges PRs; manages access | `MAINTAINERS.md`, `.github/` |

One person can hold more than one role. Every role above must be assigned before a semester's `README.md` is marked started.

## Shared duties

Every officer:

- Reads [maintenance-rules.md](../01-governance/maintenance-rules.md) once and follows it.
- Reviews PRs when asked. Any officer can review.
- Knows the [Code of Conduct](../../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) reporting steps well enough to act at an event.
- Fills in their part of `handoff.md` at semester close before leaving the role.

## Changing roles

Adding, removing, or renaming a role is a constitution amendment and a decision-log entry. Reassigning who holds a role is an election or appointment under [elections.md](elections.md).
