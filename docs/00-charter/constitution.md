# Constitution (DRAFT)

**Status: DRAFT. Not yet ratified by the membership or filed with Student Activities.** Anything marked [TBD] must be filled in before ratification. Anything marked [verify with Student Activities] is a rule this draft takes from RCC's public club pages and the Club Advisor's Guide; confirm the current wording with the Student Activities Office before filing.

Why this matters: RCC lists "a constitution and by-laws" among the requirements for a recognized club, and the Advisor's Guide says a new club submits its constitution within four weeks of its first meeting and files changes within two weeks [verify with Student Activities]. Without it on file, ASRCC recognition and funding are at risk.

Sources: <https://www.rcc.edu/life-at-rcc/clubs-and-organizations.html> and the Club Advisor's Guide linked from <https://www.rcc.edu/life-at-rcc/student-government.html>.

## Article I. Name

1. The name of this organization is Google Developer Group on Campus Riverside City College, as registered with ASRCC. The short form is GDG on Campus @ RCC.
2. Google's brand guide requires the form "Google Developer Groups on Campus [College Name]" or "GDG on Campus [College Name]" in official material. See [brand.md](../04-brand/brand.md).
3. The organization is a chapter in Google's GDG on Campus program with a chapter page at <https://gdg.community.dev/gdg-on-campus-riverside-city-college-riverside-united-states/>.

## Article II. Purpose

1. To give RCC students hands-on experience with Google technologies through workshops, talks, team projects, and program events (Solutions Challenge, Google I/O Extended, DevFest, Build with AI).
2. To connect members with working developers, Google guest speakers, and transfer institutions.
3. To run at least one published event per month of instruction. [TBD: confirm as a binding minimum or move to goals]
