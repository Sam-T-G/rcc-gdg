# Mission

## In one sentence

GDG on Campus @ RCC gives Riverside City College students a place to build with Google technologies, learn from working developers, and ship something real before they transfer or graduate.

## What we do

Google describes a GDG on Campus member as someone who can "meet local developers with similar interests in technology, attend talks on a wide range of technical topics, and learn new skills through hands-on workshops" (source: <https://developers.google.com/profile/badges/community/gdg/GDGoC/member>). At RCC that looks like:

| Activity | Example from Spring 2026 |
|---|---|
| Weekly meetings | Wednesdays, 10:00 AM, BLCIS A-103 |
| Hands-on workshops | "How to Vibe Code Like a Pro" (Mar 25, 2026); the 40-minute Modular Impact Sprint on Google AI Studio |
| Team projects | "Tech Startups and Solving Problems: Team Creation Day" (Apr 1, 2026) leading into "Million Dollar Pitch" (Apr 22, 2026) |
| Demo Day | Jun 3, 2026, the semester's closing event where teams present their projects |
| Google program events | Solutions Challenge, Google I/O Extended, DevFest, Build with AI, as each season allows |
| Guest speakers | Google guest speakers and workshops, funded through ASRCC when approved |
| Trips | Silicon Valley trip (Googleplex visitor experience, Stanford, SJSU), as funding allows |

The Spring 2026 ASRCC request planned 20 or more events per year, about 90 percent on campus, with attendance of roughly 95 percent RCC students and 5 percent faculty.

## Who it is for

Any RCC student, any major, any experience level. The events are built so that a first-semester student and someone who has already shipped an app can both leave with something.

## How we measure it

Each semester's [goals.md](../../semesters/2026-fall/goals.md) sets targets and the [retrospective](../../semesters/2026-fall/retrospective.md) reports against them. The numbers that matter:

- Events published on Bevy per month of instruction (Google describes the organizer role as hosting events "typically on a monthly basis")
- Registered versus checked-in attendance per event, from Bevy reports (see [attendance-tracking.md](../02-operations/attendance-tracking.md))
- Teams that reach Demo Day with a working prototype
- Members who take on an organizer role the following semester

## Relationship to Google and to RCC

GDG on Campus Riverside City College is an independent group; our activities and the opinions expressed here should in no way be linked to Google, the corporation. Organizers are volunteers, not Google employees or agents. The club is chartered through ASRCC and Student Activities at RCC and follows RCC's club rules (see [club-recognition-and-icc.md](../02-operations/club-recognition-and-icc.md)).
