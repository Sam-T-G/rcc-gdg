# Membership

## Who can join

Any student enrolled at Riverside City College. No major, prior experience, or Google account is required to attend a meeting. Google's own line on chapters: "All developers and those interested in Google technologies or in becoming a developer are welcome to join the GDG platform" (<https://support.google.com/developergroups/answer/2893268?hl=en>).

## What members can expect

- A published Bevy event for every meeting and workshop, with RSVP and a recording where the format allows.
- Workshops written so a first-semester student can follow along.
- A team and a project by mid-semester if you want one, and a slot at Demo Day if you finish.
- The [Code of Conduct](../../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) enforced.

## What the club asks of members

- RSVP on Bevy and check in at the door. Attendance is how the club proves itself to ASRCC and to Google (see [attendance-tracking.md](../02-operations/attendance-tracking.md)).
- Follow the Code of Conduct in person and online.
- Tell an organizer when something is not working.

## Counts, not rosters

This repo never holds a list of member names, emails, or student IDs. Each semester's `roster.md` records counts only, and says which count it is:

| Measure | Where the current number lives | Source of the number |
|---|---|---|
| Active members reported to ASRCC | the current semester's `roster.md` (for example [2026-fall/roster.md](../../semesters/2026-fall/roster.md)) | The most recent ASRCC funding request |
| Group members on Bevy | the current semester's `roster.md` | Chapter page member count, with the date it was read |
| Fee-paid RCC students on the club registration | the current semester's `roster.md` | Student Activities |

ASRCC requires at least seven RCC students who have paid the Student Services Activity Fee, including the President, Treasurer, and ICC Representative (<https://www.rcc.edu/life-at-rcc/clubs-and-organizations.html>). The Treasurer confirms this number at every recharter.

The actual roster (the Excel file the Advisor's Guide asks for) lives with Student Activities and the advisor, not here. See [privacy-and-public-repo-policy.md](../01-governance/privacy-and-public-repo-policy.md).

## Leaving

Members can leave the Bevy chapter and Discord at any time. Removal for conduct reasons follows the Code of Conduct.
